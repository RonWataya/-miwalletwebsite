In commercial use in Blantyre

OmniPOS

Point of sale, inventory and tax filing for retail. A shop records what it buys in and what it sells, and from those two actions OmniPOS tells the owner what was spent, what was made on each item, and what is owed to the Malawi Revenue Authority in VAT. It then files the return directly through MRA's Electronic Invoicing System.

The till keeps trading when the internet drops and syncs when the connection returns. It is built for owners with no accounting background, and it runs on whatever device a shop already has, down to a basic smartphone.

Inventory management, from one shop to a whole warehouse

Most of a retailer's money is sitting on shelves and in storerooms. OmniPOS follows every item from the moment it is bought in to the moment it is sold, written off or corrected, so the owner always knows what the stock is worth, what is running low and what is close to expiry.

For large stores and warehouses we are building RFID stock control into OmniPOS. Stock carries a tag, staff read it with a handheld device instead of counting it by hand, and deliveries update the inventory as they are received.

How inventory and RFID work

  • Stock in, stock out

    Every delivery and every sale moves the count, so the figure on screen matches the shelf.

  • Mistakes and losses kept apart

    A correction undoes a wrong entry. A write-off records damaged or expired stock as a loss.

  • Counts in minutes, not days

    With RFID, a whole aisle is read in one walk rather than counted item by item.

In use at car hire firms in Malawi

Car Hire Management System & Car Tracking

A complete system for running a car hire business: customer registration and licence checks, quotations, bookings, invoicing, payments and security deposits, vehicle release and return with photographic condition records, live vehicle tracking, servicing history, certificate expiry and a controlled store.

Everything to do with one hire sits on a single record, so nothing is chased between the front desk, the finance office and the gate.

Mi Wallet Pay

Our payment platform, and the project the company was founded on. It lets a person pay from their phone by scanning a QR code, by tapping the phone against a terminal using NFC, or by tapping a physical tag. Three methods, so that almost any customer can be served whatever handset they carry.
