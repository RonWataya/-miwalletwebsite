Pay from your phone, whatever phone you carry.

Mi Wallet Pay is our payment platform and the project the company was founded on. Scan a QR code, tap your phone, or tap a tag: three ways to pay, so almost anyone can be served.

How it works Join the pilot

Three ways to pay

Not every customer carries the same phone. Mi Wallet Pay offers three methods so a shop can take payment from nearly everyone who walks in.

Scan a QR code

The customer scans the shop's code with the app and confirms the amount. It works on any smartphone with a camera.

Tap your phone with NFC

On phones that support NFC, the customer taps the phone against the terminal. No code to scan, no card to hand over.

Tap a physical tag

For customers whose handset supports neither, a physical tag linked to their wallet does the same job with a single tap.

How a payment works, step by step

  1. Open a wallet

    A customer registers in the Mi Wallet Pay app and gets their own wallet.

  2. Top up by card

    The wallet is loaded with a Visa or Mastercard through our own payment gateway.

  3. Pay at the counter

    At a shop, the customer pays by QR code, NFC or tag, whichever suits their handset.

  4. The sale is recorded

    Mi Wallet Pay is designed to connect directly with OmniPOS, so a wallet payment is recorded against the sale like any other payment method.
