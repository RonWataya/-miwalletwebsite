Custom software

A selection of the systems we have been engaged to build.

Fluxstr8

Cold chain monitoring system

An Android application that tracks the temperature of pharmaceutical products as they move between regions, so a consignment's cold chain is monitored across the whole journey rather than checked only on arrival. Temperature integrity is the difference between usable medicine and waste, and the system makes a break in it visible.

GIZ

District Nutrition Coordination System

A coordination platform for nutrition programme activity at district level. It brings reporting and coordination into one system, so programme data can be collected, seen and acted on rather than assembled by hand from separate returns.

AfriTicket

NFC payment system for commuters

A contactless fare payment system for public transport. Commuters pay by tapping rather than handling cash, which is faster at the point of boarding and leaves an accurate record of every fare collected.

Alongside these we build web applications, mobile applications, internal business systems and integrations for clients across sectors, including the Car Hire Management System now used by car hire firms in Malawi.

How an engagement works

From the first conversation to a system your staff use every day.

  1. We come and see how you work

    We are in Blantyre, so we can sit with the people who will use the system. We look for where work is repeated, where errors creep in and where information gets lost between departments.

  2. We agree scope, timeline and cost in writing

    You receive a written proposal setting out what will be built, by when and for how much. If requirements change along the way, we agree the effect on time and price before we act on it.

  3. We build and test with your staff

    You see working software early and often. Your staff test it against real cases, and the rules that prevent the mistakes we found in step one are built in.

  4. We hand it over, and you own it

    Where we develop a system to your order, you own it: source code, database and all. We would rather earn your maintenance and hosting on merit each year than hold your operation hostage.

  5. We host and look after it, if you want us to

    Your system can run on our infrastructure, with monitoring, backups and updates handled by us, or be installed wherever you choose.

Hosting and infrastructure

Before we sold software we were hosting other companies' operations, and we still do. Clients have trusted us with their websites, their company email and their business data since 2021.

All of it runs on Amazon Web Services infrastructure that Mi Wallet procures and configures. We are not reselling somebody else's shared hosting. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, each client is kept separate from every other, backups are automated and tested, and patching and monitoring are our responsibility rather than yours.
