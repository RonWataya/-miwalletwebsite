Built for the way shops here trade

Systems designed elsewhere assume reliable electricity, reliable internet and expensive hardware. OmniPOS does not.

It keeps selling when the internet drops

The till works offline and saves every sale on the device. When the connection returns, everything syncs to your account.

It runs on the device you already have

A phone, a tablet or a computer can be your till, down to a basic smartphone. No special machine is needed to start.

No accounting knowledge needed

OmniPOS speaks in plain terms: money in, money out and your balance. Every sale, payment, refund and expense is recorded as it happens.

VAT filed straight to MRA

OmniPOS works out the VAT on your sales and files the return directly through MRA's Electronic Invoicing System, without a separate fiscal device.

A trading day with OmniPOS, step by step

What happens from setting up your shop to filing your VAT, in the order you will do it.

  1. Set up your shop

    Create your account, enter your business details and set your VAT rate. Add the staff who will use the till; each person signs in with their own login, so every sale shows who made it.

  2. Add your products

    Enter each item with its barcode, buying price and selling price. From then on, scanning or searching finds it instantly at the till. How inventory works

  3. Record stock as it arrives

    Each delivery is entered with its supplier, quantity and cost. OmniPOS keeps count of your stock and of how much money you have put into it.

  4. Sell at the till

    Scan or search for items, and take payment by cash, Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba, card or bank transfer. Each payment is recorded against its method, so the cash drawer and the mobile money balances can be checked separately at the end of the day.

  5. Give credit where you need to

    Customers who buy on credit get their own running account. Payments they bring in clear their oldest debts first, and you can show them exactly what they owe.

  6. Keep an eye on stock

    Every sale reduces the stock count on its own. OmniPOS warns you when items are running low or nearing expiry, and records damaged or expired goods as losses.

  7. Close the day knowing where you stand

    See how much money came in, how much went out, your balance, what sold fast and what may run out soon. The profit on each item is already calculated.

  8. File your VAT with MRA

    Because every sale is already recorded with its VAT, OmniPOS can file the return directly through MRA's Electronic Invoicing System. The figures you file are the figures your shop actually traded.

  9. Ask the business companion what it means

    The built-in assistant reads your sales, stock and money records and explains them in plain words. It can produce financial statements, a balance sheet and projections, point out unusual changes early, and answer questions you ask about your own business. It uses only your data.
